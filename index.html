How I think
"Creativity opens the door. Operating discipline keeps it open."
Most restaurants don't fail because the idea wasn't good enough. They fail because the idea wasn't backed by recipes, costings, training, prep systems, clear roles and honest weekly controls. I'm not the person who dims the creative ambition. I'm the person who makes sure it can actually survive contact with reality.
"Busy is not the same as progress."
A full day can feel productive — suppliers chased, prep corrected, service fixed. But if the same problems come back next week, the business didn't get stronger. It just kept moving. I look for what keeps repeating and what needs to change so next Monday is easier.
"Can it travel?"
The test I apply to every operating model is whether it works outside the building — when the team is in an unfamiliar environment, the founder isn't watching, and conditions are imperfect. Strong standards can travel. Workarounds can't.
"Vision creates momentum. Operational discipline turns it into sustainable performance."
I've worked with founders whose businesses were falling apart because no one had written the standards down, built the accountability structures, or made the financial controls real. My job is to turn the vision into something the organisation can actually deliver.
